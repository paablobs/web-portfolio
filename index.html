// About

I'm a full stack developer with a frontend focus. Since 2020, I've worked on web interfaces, backend services and business integrations. I enjoy frontend architecture and UI work, and I'm equally willing to work on APIs, databases and end-to-end features.

At We Are Psh, I work with React, Redux and TypeScript, modernize AngularJS screens, and contribute to microfrontends and a shared component library. On the backend, I develop and maintain NestJS services and REST APIs connected to MySQL, alongside Docker and CI/CD workflows.

Previously, at Futit Services, I integrated Jira and Scaleway with Openbravo/Etendo and developed WebPOS interfaces. That work involved databases, business workflows and direct client support.

My personal projects explore local-first applications, browser-based databases, optional cloud synchronization and automated testing. Their case studies explain the implementation choices and current limitations, not just the technology stack.
